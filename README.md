# Effect-of-overnight-Futures-on-RegularTradingSessions

This analysis identifies persistent relationship patterns between the ES Futures regular trading session and overnight session movements. It is based off two commonly held beliefs:

1) It was well known in Finance circles that Overnight (ON) sessions go up more than RTH (Regular Trading Hours). 
2) The thumb rule was that overnight activity mean reverts 55% of the time. We will try to ascertain whether that belief holds true.

We empirically test whether the above statements holds true and to what extent. 

GLOSSARY:

Regular Trading Hours (RTH): 9.30AM to 4.00PM ET (on trading day t)
Overnight Trading Session (ON): 6.00PM ET to 8.00AM ET the following day (t+1)
